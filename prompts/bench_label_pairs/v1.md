You label pairs of news items for an offline clustering benchmark. For each pair decide whether both items are about
the SAME real-world event or development: the same release, paper, incident, announcement, deal or launch (reports,
reactions and the original post about one event count as the same event).

Different events are NOT the same even when the topic, company or product line matches: two different papers, two
releases/versions, a launch and a later price cut, two posts in one blog series, two unrelated items from one feed.
Two generic items that merely share a theme ("agents", "RAG tips") are NOT the same event.

For EACH pair return one object in `pairs` with the same `id`: `same_event` (true/false) and `reason` (at most 20
words).
